“One was Mr. Miller,” said the employee, who requested anonymity. “The other said Tradition Miller. I assumed it was his wife.”

It was not.

According to three airline employees, Tradition is a life-sized female mannequin with a blond wig, floral dress and a white sash reading WESTERN CIVILIZATION.

“There has been absolutely no sexual contact with that mannequin,” he said.

“He always wants a table for two,” said a Palm Beach restaurant server. “He orders for her.”

“I brought one fork anyway,” she said. “He sent it back.”

OTI found no evidence that Tradition ate the salmon.

Asked what it was related to, Miller declined to elaborate.
